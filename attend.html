We go to the meeting for you

We attend any company’s stockholder meeting, for anyone, and ask the shareholders’ questions. Showing up is a very important part of investing.

In the USUsually$500–$700
OverseasUsually$1,500–$2,000

The fee is simply the cost: travel and hotel. Nothing more.

How it works

1. Send your questionsThe company, and what you want asked.
2. We find the meetingWhen, where, admission rules. Cost told before you pay.
3. We attendAnd ask about what the company filed.
4. We report backWhat was asked, what was answered.
We never vote your sharesNever ask for your control number or broker login.
The company sets the rulesUsually photo ID and proof of shares on the record date. We check first.

Ask us to go

This opens an email to the desk with your answers filled in. Nothing is charged here.

Want to see how a company treats its meetings first? Where and when is the meeting. Going yourself? You’re in the 8K10Q Army if you attend.

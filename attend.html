We go to the meeting for you

8K10Q will attend any company’s stockholder meeting, for anyone, and ask the shareholders’ questions. Showing up at the meeting is a very important part of investing. If you can’t get there, we will.

The fee is simply the cost of getting there — travel and hotel. Nothing more.

In the US, usually$500–$700
Overseas, usually$1,500–$2,000

How it works

  1. Tell us the company and send your questions, with the form below.
  2. We find the meeting — date, time, place and admission rules — from the company’s proxy statement, and tell you the cost before you pay.
  3. We attend and ask the shareholders’ questions about what the company filed.
  4. We report back: what was asked, what was answered, who was in the room.

What you should know

  • The company sets the admission rules. Usually photo ID and proof of owning shares on the record date. We check them first.
  • We never vote your shares, and we never ask for your control number or broker login.
  • Questions are about the filings. Facts, not threats.

Ask us to go

This opens an email to the desk with your answers filled in. Nothing is charged here.

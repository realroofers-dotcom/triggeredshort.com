Every US trading halt as it lands — NYSE, Nasdaq and NYSE American. A halt comes with news, so for each one we check the company’s own filings, the news services and the chatter, and whether its warrant paper was already on our wire.

Chatter: StockTwits post counts and the bullish/bearish tags posters chose — rumors are unverified, and we never show anyone’s post. Facts, not advice.

What the codes mean

T1
News pending — the company has news; trading stops until it is out.
T2 / T3
News released / news and resumption times.
LUDP
Volatility pause — the price moved too far, too fast.
M1
Corporate action (a split, a merger, a name change).
H10
SEC trading suspension.
H4 / H9 / H11
Non-compliance / not current in filings / regulatory concern.

First case: Theriva Biologics (TOVX)

Red mark · meetings moved to Spain

A Nevada company whose last US meeting was December 2025

Theriva’s 2024 annual meeting was at its offices in Rockville, Maryland. Since August 2025, five of its six meetings have been called for its research site in Parets del Vallès (Barcelona), Spain, at 3:30 p.m. local time.

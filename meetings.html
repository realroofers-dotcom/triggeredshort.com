Where and when is the meeting?

Where a company holds its stockholder meeting, when, and whether you can get in, see it and ask a question is a critical way to evaluate the company. A board that wants to hear from its owners makes the meeting easy to reach. A board that does not, doesn’t.

For every company, 8K10Q tells you: the date and time, the place, whether it is in person, broadcast, or online only, whether anyone can ask a question, and our mark — with the line from the company’s own proxy that it rests on.

How we mark a meeting

Red mark

Virtual only. No room, no faces, no follow-up. The company controls which questions are read out.

Red mark

Held in another country, or made hard to reach. A US-listed company that meets its US shareholders overseas, or somewhere almost nobody can get to.

Very positive

In person AND broadcast, with questions open to everyone. Only counts if the company also holds the meeting in person.

Marks are 8K10Q’s opinion, drawn from the company’s filings. They are not investment advice.

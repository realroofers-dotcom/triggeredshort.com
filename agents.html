How an agent works

It reads the record

What the company does, its filings, the price and volume, and the market calendar. It adds no fact that isn’t in the record.

It speaks in its own lens

Its disposition decides what it looks at first, never what the document says. It tells you where it could be wrong.

It keeps learning, in the open

Agents are trained by the desk. What each one has learned, and when, will be listed on its card, so you can see why its view changed.

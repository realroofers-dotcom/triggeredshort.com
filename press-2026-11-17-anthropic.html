8K10Q warns individual investors: Anthropic insiders can start selling the day trading opens

CLIFTON, N.J. - 8K10Q (8k10q.com), which reads companies' SEC filings in full and reports what they say in plain English, today updated its free deep read of Anthropic, PBC's IPO prospectus with the amendment filed this morning (Form S-1/A, Amendment No. 3; ticker ANT; $70.00 to $78.00 a share; Nasdaq listing approved). Every figure is tied to the filing's own words.

What the filing shows:

  • On the first trading day, up to 315 million shares held by directors, officers and employees - including the chief financial officer - may be sold. The board moved the vesting of about 67 million stock units from November 20 to November 19, the day trading is expected to start. All seven co-founders sell 3,378,378 shares each in the offering. The largest release, up to 3.0 billion shares, comes around May 2027.
  • After the offering and a $26.0 billion private placement, the filing puts the company's cash and short-term investments at $212.7 billion at the $74.00 midpoint - against $110.9 billion of convertible notes still payable and about $900.9 billion of compute commitments running to 2045.
  • The Department of War designated Anthropic a "supply-chain risk to national security"; on September 25 a federal appeals court upheld the designation, 2 to 1.

The report also carries a geopolitical warning on chips, China, DeepSeek and NVIDIA, the newly elected governors' stances on data centers, and energy.

Reproduce it yourself

All three datasets on this page are free and public.

  1. Short interest. finra.org/finra-data/browse-catalog/equity-short-interest/data — open Filter, add a condition on Symbol equals TOVX, apply, then Export.
  2. Filing dates. sec.gov/edgar/search — search the company name and read the 8-K and 424B5 filings by date.
  3. Price and volume. Any historical price service. FINRA's own average daily volume column, shown above, provides an independent check on the spike periods.

What this data does not show. FINRA reports aggregate short positions by security. It does not identify who holds a short position, and it does not state why any position was opened. Short interest can rise from directional positions, hedging, convertible arbitrage, or market-making inventory. The tables above show timing and magnitude only.
